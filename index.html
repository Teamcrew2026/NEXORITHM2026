ABOUT THE SYMPOSIUM

What is Nexorithm 2026?

Nexorithm 2026 is the premier annual technical symposium organized jointly by the Department of Computer Science & Engineering (CSE) and Artificial Intelligence & Data Science (AIDS) at Infant Jesus College of Engineering. It serves as an interactive platform for aspiring engineers to exchange groundbreaking ideas, test their algorithmic prowess, collaborate on emerging technologies, and compete across 10 diverse technical and creative domains.

Algorithmic Excellence & Problem Solving

Competitive programming, code debugging, and software craftsmanship.

AI, UI/UX & Creative Tech Frontiers

Generative AI prompt engineering, rapid web interface design, research defense, and esports.

ABOUT THE INSTITUTION

Infant Jesus College of Engineering

Infant Jesus College of Engineering (IJCE), situated in Keelavallanadu on the Tirunelveli–Thoothukudi Highway (NH-7A), is a reputed technical institution approved by AICTE, New Delhi, and affiliated with Anna University, Chennai.

The Departments of CSE & AIDS are equipped with modern computing laboratories, high-speed campus networking, specialized research clusters for Artificial Intelligence, Data Analytics, and Cloud Systems, fostering holistic academic excellence and industry readiness.
